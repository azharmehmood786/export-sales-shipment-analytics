# KPI Definitions

## 1. Total Export Volume
Total quantity of products exported during a selected period.

## 2. Average Order Value (AOV)
Average sales value per export order.

Formula:
Total Export Sales / Number of Export Orders

## 3. On-Time Shipment Rate
Percentage of export orders shipped on or before the promised shipment date.

Formula:
(On-Time Orders / Total Shipped Orders) * 100
